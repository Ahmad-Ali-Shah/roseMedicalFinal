## Goodwill and brand protection

- Public logo, typography, colours, spacing, grid, motion, components, navigation and templates remain locked.
- All editable public changes follow Draft → Review → Public Preview → Explicit Publish.
- Revision history remains intact; rollback creates a new revision.
- Placeholder, duplicate-code, missing-media, Arabic-completeness, crop and alt-text warnings are visible.
- Certification, manufacturing, factory, ownership, award, export, legal and clinical claims are isolated from ordinary publishing.
- Inquiries and general messages remain separate.
- No page builder, arbitrary HTML/CSS, theme controls, public prices, payments, orders, stock, inventory, shipping, discounts, ratings or revenue controls appear.
